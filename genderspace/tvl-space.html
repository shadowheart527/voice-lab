Weight is the spectral roll-off of your harmonics (flat = heavy/buzzy, steep = light), noise-gated and anchored against the video's own light and heavy demonstrations. Size is your R1/R2 position between the masc and fem formant anchors, small at the top. Full = weight matched to size; hollow = lighter than your size; buzzy = heavier than your size. Absolute placement shifts a little with mic and room; trust contrasts within your own voice most. To pin the weight scale to your own mic: make your lightest and your heaviest sound, note the two dB/oct readings above, and reload with ?wlight=<lightest>&wheavy=<heaviest> in the address.
